Leaving the Army soon? Explore rent, deposits, moving costs, and temporary housing around the DMV or Houston. Some programs help before separation; others require veteran status, a financial hardship, or risk of homelessness.

A few terms, explained simply

Adaptive sports: Sports with equipment or activities adjusted for a person’s disability or injury.

Service dog: A dog trained to do specific tasks related to a person’s disability. A dog that only provides comfort or companionship is not a service dog under the ADA. Read the ADA explanation ›

PTSD: Posttraumatic stress disorder. The programs listed offer different kinds of help, such as clinical care, counseling, or peer connection.

Referral: A provider or program sends you to another service. Waitlist: You may qualify, but there may not be an opening yet.

How to use this guide

A status filter identifies programs worth checking, not a determination of eligibility. Open the source before applying. Disability ratings, discharge, dependents, service dates, command approval, and residency can change the answer.

Keeping the guide current

Program information is a dated research snapshot. Review dates describe individual source checks. This public demo has no background update service; verify availability with the provider.
